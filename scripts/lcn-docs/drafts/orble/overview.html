Orble.online is a geography guessing game on a live 3D globe. You identify missing countries or cities; warmer map colors and a Radius list show how close each guess is. Play solo or host a party with a shareable code.

How it plays

  • Guess countries or cities - autocomplete helps you lock the name.
  • Heat colors on the globe plus the Radius sidebar (km/miles, closest-first) show distance and direction.
  • Round results award base points, Perfect bonuses, and XP toward longer sessions.
  • Parties: host gets a code, friends join, then Play when the lobby is ready.
